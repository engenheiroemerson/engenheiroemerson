## 🚀 Featured Projects

### ☀️ Intelligent Dual-Axis Solar Tracking System
> **Maximizing Solar Energy Generation Through Intelligent Tracking and Automation**

Maximizing the efficiency of photovoltaic systems is one of the key challenges in renewable energy engineering. This academic project focuses on the design and development of an intelligent dual-axis solar tracking system that automatically adjusts the orientation of photovoltaic panels to follow the sun throughout the day. The project integrates embedded systems, sensors, automation, and control strategies to optimize energy generation while supporting more efficient and sustainable energy solutions.

* **Technologies:** Solar Energy Systems • Renewable Energy Engineering • Embedded Systems • Sensors and Automation • Microcontrollers • Control Systems • Energy Efficiency • Electrical Power Systems

---

### 🌐 IoT-Based Air Quality Monitoring System
> **Enabling Intelligent Environmental Monitoring Through Connected Sensor Technologies**

Air quality monitoring plays a vital role in protecting public health and promoting sustainable urban development. This academic project focuses on the design and development of an Internet of Things (IoT)-based environmental monitoring system capable of measuring air quality through integrated sensors and transmitting real-time data to cloud platforms for analysis and visualization. The project integrates embedded systems, wireless communication, cloud computing, and data analytics to support intelligent environmental monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

* **Technologies:** Internet of Things (IoT) • Embedded Systems • Environmental Monitoring • Air Quality Sensors • Wireless Communication • Cloud Computing • Data Acquisition • Data Analytics

---

### 💡 Intelligent Street Lighting System
> **Advancing Smart Cities Through Intelligent Lighting and Energy Optimization**

Energy-efficient public lighting is a key component of modern smart cities. This academic project focuses on the design and development of an intelligent street lighting system that automatically adjusts illumination based on pedestrian and vehicle detection. By integrating embedded systems, sensors, automation, and wireless communication, the project aims to improve energy efficiency, optimize operational performance, and support the development of smarter and more sustainable urban infrastructure.

* **Technologies:** Smart Grid Technologies • Embedded Systems • Arduino Programming • Sensors and Automation • Energy Efficiency • Intelligent Lighting Systems • Power Electronics • Smart City Technologies
